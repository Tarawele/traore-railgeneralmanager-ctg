Research Summary

Organization of passenger transport on the Transguinean railway

Context and Objectives

This research, conducted at the Russian University of Transport (MIIT), proposes a complete operational model for deploying passenger services on the Transguinean railway infrastructure (670 km). It addresses a critical need for territorial connectivity in Guinea.

Main objectives:

  • Develop an integrated passenger transport system on the CTG corridor
  • Optimize the use of existing infrastructure
  • Create a viable economic model for passenger transport

Methodology and Approach

Demand Analysis

Adapted gravitational model using Guinea's demographic and socio-economic data to predict passenger flows.

Railway Capacity

Saturation calculations according to UIC standards with a reduction coefficient ε=1.26 for freight/passenger traffic integration.

Cost Optimization

Comparative analysis of 4 train formation schemes to identify the most economical solution (1,511 RUB/passenger).

Sizing

Application of international standards for station capacity calculations with adjustment coefficients k1-k4.

Key Results

  • 18 trains/day planned on 40 relations between 8 key cities
  • Capture of 10% initial modal share in the corridor
  • Optimal scheme: 10 coaches hauled at 80.3 km/h commercial speed
  • Implementation of 8 main stations and 12 halts
  • Validation of platform lengths (481m < 1250m)

Operational Roadmap

Phase 1 — Launch

4 intercity trains/day on the Conakry ↔ Kérouané axis, timed to align with mining freight flows.

Phase 2 — Densification

Integration of 14 regional trains and development of intermodality with Conakry International Airport.
